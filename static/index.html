BookMyShow Ticket Monitor

local/bookmyshow-monitor

Watches a movie in your city, or one theatre, and pings you on Telegram, ntfy and this page the moment tickets open, a new date or show appears, or a sold-out show frees up.

Input

Paste a BookMyShow movie or buytickets URL, an ET… code, or type a name and press Find.
BookMyShow city slug, as in the URL.
Every word must appear in the venue name. Separate alternatives with commas: PVR Velachery, INOX Chennai Citi. Leave empty to alert when the city opens.
YYYYMMDD, comma-separated.
Only needed for cities not listed.
None selected means any time.

How it works

  1. Calls BookMyShow's own showtimes endpoint for the movie, the same data the buytickets page uses.
  2. Reads date chips (open or not yet open), venues, showtimes and seat-class availability.
  3. Filters by your theatre, dates and time of day.
  4. Alerts on: first opening, new date, new show, sold-out → available.
  5. If Cloudflare blocks plain requests, it switches to a real browser automatically.
